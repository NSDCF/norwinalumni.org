This spring, representatives of the Norwin Alumni & Friends Association joined students and staff for the Peer Buddy Celebration honoring members of the graduating Class of 2026 in Norwin's life skills and autistic support programs.

The celebration gave students a chance to be recognized for the work, growth and accomplishments that marked their years at Norwin. For NAFA, it was also an opportunity to support students at an important transition point as they prepared to graduate and move on to what comes next.

Events like the Peer Buddy Celebration help make sure that every student's achievements are acknowledged as part of the larger Norwin school community. They also create a setting where students, staff and community organizations can come together to recognize the people and relationships that have shaped the school year.

NAFA was pleased to join this year's celebration and recognize the Class of 2026 as they reached the end of their time at Norwin. We look forward to returning next spring to celebrate the Class of 2027 and the accomplishments they will bring with them to graduation.
